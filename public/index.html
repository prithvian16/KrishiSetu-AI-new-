One app + three website interfaces

📱 Farmer App

The complete farmer journey runs inside a mobile-app simulator: registration, crop intelligence, optional pre-arrival quality check, centre recommendation, booking, live queue, notifications, payment tracking and language switching.

🖥️ Procurement Centre

Website-only operations for capacity, live queue, check-in, calling farmers, procurement completion and payment initiation.

🔎 Inspector

Website-only official physical verification. This is deliberately separated from the farmer's optional pre-arrival quality check.

📊 Government

Website-only monitoring for all-centre visibility, congestion, utilisation, procurement, payment and queue analytics.

Prototype boundary: government bank sync, AI crop assessment, SMS/IVR and payment are simulated. Production would use authorised integrations, authentication, RBAC, encryption and validated AI models.
