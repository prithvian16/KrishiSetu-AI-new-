# Suggested SIH demo flow

1. Home → **Start farmer journey**.
2. Farmer signup/login happens inside the **Kisan mobile app simulator**.
3. Register Wheat/Paddy and quantity.
4. Choose **Optional pre-arrival quality check** → upload crop photo → show preliminary A/B/C advisory result.
5. Continue to smart centre recommendation.
6. Book slot → receive token and ETA.
7. Open Queue → demonstrate live queue and check-in.
8. Switch from the app to the **website-only Inspector Dashboard**.
9. Inspector records final physical verification → accepted.
10. Switch back to the Kisan app → **Payment** tab shows processing payment.
11. Mark payment paid from the website workflow → refresh app → farmer sees **Payment credited**.
12. Switch to Government Dashboard to show system-wide centre/queue/payment monitoring.
13. Change app language mid-journey to demonstrate anytime language support.

Pitch line:
> “We are not only giving farmers a token. We reduce uncertainty before the farmer travels, then connect that decision to live queue management, official verification and payment tracking.”
